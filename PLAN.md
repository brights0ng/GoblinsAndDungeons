Phase 2: Item Overhaul (1.20.5 / 1.21.1)In 1.18.2, items like the Magic Staff, Goblin Crown, and Rings likely used ItemStack.getOrCreateTag(), hardcoded UUID attribute modifiers, and enum-based ArmorMaterial. In NeoForge 1.21.1:ArmorMaterial is now a Registry Entry: We will register the Goblin Crown's material to Registries.ARMOR_MATERIAL and pass Holder<ArmorMaterial> to ArmorItem.ItemAttributeModifiers & UUID Removal: 1.21 replaced UUID on AttributeModifier with ResourceLocation (e.g., ResourceLocation.fromNamespaceAndPath(MODID, "ring_of_health_boost")). This actually makes the Ring of Health (+1 Heart in off-hand) and Potion of Ogre's Might (+3 Attack, -10% Speed) much cleaner to write.   Custom NBT $\rightarrow$ DataComponentType: Any state stored on items (like staff cooldowns or mimic/urn item data) will be converted to registered DataComponentType records with Codec and StreamCodec.B. Phase 3 & 4: Entities & Rendering (Vanilla vs. GeckoLib check)First check we will do on the source: Did 1.18.2 Goblins & Dungeons use vanilla EntityModel / LayerDefinition (which superlord9362 often uses in mods like Prehistoric Fauna) or GeckoLib?If Vanilla ModelPart: Porting is super smooth—we just update renderToBuffer(PoseStack, VertexConsumer, int, int, int) (in 1.21, the r, g, b, a floats were packed into a single int color ARGB parameter!) and change Vector3f rotations to JOML's Axis.XP.rotationDegrees(...).If GeckoLib: We add GeckoLib 4 for NeoForge 1.21.1 and migrate IAnimatable $\rightarrow$ GeoEntity with AnimatableInstanceCache.Entity Data & Networking: SynchedEntityData.defineSynchedData() in 1.21.1 now takes SynchedEntityData.Builder builder instead of calling this.entityData.define(...). Custom packets (if the Goblin King or Soul Bullet uses SimpleChannel) will be converted to NeoForge's CustomPacketPayload + PayloadRegistrar (matching how SeismicNetwork and TierMapPayloads work in your repo).
